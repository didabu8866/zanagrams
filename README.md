## 🌟 What is Zanagrams?

Zanagrams is a daily word puzzle game combining elements of anagrams, word search, and brain teasers. Every day, we present players with a carefully crafted 16-letter grid puzzle designed to challenge vocabulary skills and strategic thinking.

**Key Features**:
- 🆓 **100% Free** - No ads, no subscriptions, no paywalls
- 🧩 **Daily Challenges** - New puzzle every day
- 📱 **Play Anywhere** - Works on mobile, tablet, and desktop
- 🌙 **Dark Mode** - Comfortable playing experience
- 🧠 **Brain Training** - Educational and entertaining
